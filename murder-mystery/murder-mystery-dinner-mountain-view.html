Your host

Hosted by Ritika, founder of 23rd Parallel.

23rd Parallel is an intimate Bay Area supper club where food, place and storytelling meet around one table. For this one-night experience, the supper becomes the stage: a royal menu, twenty characters and a mystery woven through the evening.

Before you arrive

Frequently asked questions.

Can I come alone?

Yes. Solo guests are welcome. Every guest receives a character and becomes part of the same story.

Do I need acting experience?

No. Your character information will guide you, and the evening is designed to be playful and social.

Will I know my character beforehand?

Yes. After payment, your character and mystery details are sent privately to your inbox.

Do I have to dress up?

The theme is Royal Indian Glamour — Maharajas, Maharanis and vintage Indian evening style — and guests are encouraged to dress for their role.

Can couples or groups attend?

Yes. Come solo, as a couple or with friends. Everyone participates in the same unfolding mystery.

Is wine included?

Yes. Unlimited wine is included in the $110 experience.

Where is the venue?

The dinner takes place at a private venue in Mountain View, California. The exact address is shared with confirmed guests after payment.
