23RD PARALLEL — PHOTO CONTROLS

You can change photos without editing index.html.

Upload/replace these files inside the images folder:

hero.jpg                 Homepage hero
host.jpg                 Your photo beside “A life lived along the Tropic of Cancer”
current-event.jpg        Current event / Murder Mystery image
food-01.jpg ... food-08.jpg   Food-only gallery
experience-supper.jpg    Supper Club Tables
experience-community.jpg Gather & Connect
experience-creative.jpg  Creative Collaborations
private-events.jpg       Private Events
corporate-events.jpg     Corporate Experiences

IMPORTANT: Keep the exact filename when replacing a photo. GitHub Pages will update automatically after the commit.
If host.jpg, current-event.jpg, or a food image is missing, the website shows a tasteful placeholder instead of a broken image.
